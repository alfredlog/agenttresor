Notary

Enter the admin token above.

The notary is the operator's own small fee wallet. When entries are left unanchored and nothing has happened for 2 minutes, it anchors the log head itself, so every event ends up on BSV even without payments. A top-up pays anchoring fees only: the notary can write audit anchors and nothing else. It holds no vault key and never touches company or agent funds, and it never holds more than 1000000 sats.
